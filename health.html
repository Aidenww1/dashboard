Pre-bloodwork checklist
  • Fast 10-12h (water and black coffee OK) for lipids and glucose
  • No training 48h before — lifting inflates liver enzymes (ALT/AST) and CK
  • On injectables: test at trough, note hours since last injection
  • Hydrate normally — dehydration inflates hematocrit
  • Sleep normally the night before; bad sleep skews glucose and cortisol
  • List every supplement and compound to disclose (biotin can distort thyroid panels)
  • Ask the lab for: CBC, lipids, liver, kidney, glucose/HbA1c, testosterone, estradiol (sensitive), TSH
