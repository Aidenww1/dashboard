Where your data lives
  • This device — everything you log lives in your browser (localStorage) and, for progress photos, IndexedDB.
  • Supabase (your private project) — a synced copy in app_state so your devices stay in sync, plus per-metric health tables fed by Tasker, and events.
  • Anthropic — only the specific text/image you submit for an AI analysis is sent, per request. Not stored there by this app.
Special-category data
Health, bloodwork, supplements, skin, and finance data are sensitive. They're never shared — the only copies are on your devices and in your own Supabase project.
Delete everything
Wipes all local data (localStorage + photo store) on this device and deletes the synced app_state rows in Supabase so it can't sync back. Health-metric tables aren't touched here. This cannot be undone.
