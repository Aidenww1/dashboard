Privacy & Data

Where your data lives

  • This device — everything you log is stored in your browser (localStorage) and, for progress photos, IndexedDB.
  • Supabase (your private project) — a synced copy in the app_state table so your devices stay in sync, plus per-metric health tables fed by Tasker, and events.
  • Anthropic — only the specific text/image you submit for an AI analysis is sent, per request. Not stored there by this app.

What's special-category

Health, bloodwork, supplements, skin, and finance data are sensitive. They are not shared with anyone; the only copies are on your devices and in your own Supabase project.

Delete everything

Wipes all local data (localStorage + photo store) on this device and deletes the synced app_state rows in Supabase so it can't sync back. Health-metric tables are not touched here. This cannot be undone.
